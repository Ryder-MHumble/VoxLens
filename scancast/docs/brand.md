# VoxLens Brand System

## Product Name

**VoxLens**

- **Vox**: creator voices, audience comments, spoken transcripts and public sentiment.
- **Lens**: visual understanding, video evidence, source inspection and decision focus.

VoxLens is meant to feel like a research lens for the creator web: not a text-only search box, but a system that can watch, listen, compare and cite.

## Slogan

- Chinese main title: **研究，不止文字**
- English line: **Research beyond text**

## Positioning

VoxLens is a multimodal social-video DeepResearch agent. It searches Bilibili, Douyin, YouTube, Xiaohongshu and Zhihu, samples videos/comments/transcripts, then produces a structured report with original source links and citation IDs.

## Brand Principles

- **Multimodal first**: videos, comments, subtitles and metadata are all research material.
- **Evidence over summary**: every important statement should be traceable to a source.
- **Cross-platform contrast**: Bilibili, Douyin, YouTube, Xiaohongshu and Zhihu are treated as different evidence environments.
- **Decision-grade output**: show consensus, disagreement, missing evidence and source reliability.
- **Respectful crawling**: prefer existing browser sessions or user-provided cookies; never return cookies in API responses.

## Visual Direction

- **Mood**: soft aurora research canvas, translucent glass cards, pale sky light, blush/violet gradients and quiet evidence signals.
- **Logo concept**: a folded V-shaped lens/prism around a play pupil, with small voice bars and one coral citation node; it should read as watch + listen + cite, not an ocean drop or magnifying glass.
- **Core colors**:
  - Ink plum: `#201A36`
  - Paper: `#FBFDFF`
  - Aurora sky: `#79D7FF`
  - Prism violet: `#C28DFF`
  - Creator blush: `#FF9FCF`
  - Citation coral: `#FF667C`
- **Typography**:
  - Latin UI: Inter / Manrope
  - Display: Instrument Serif / Cormorant Garamond
  - Chinese: Noto Sans SC / Source Han Sans SC
  - Numeric/UI stats: Space Grotesk

## Product Voice

VoxLens should sound precise, calm and source-aware. It should avoid absolute buying advice when evidence is weak, and explicitly say when a result is based on demo fallback, missing login state or limited comments.
